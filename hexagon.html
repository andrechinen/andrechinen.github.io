Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Hexagon & More Games developed by André Felipe Chinen handles your data. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information collected while using our applications.

1. Information We Collect

Hexagon & More Games does not require account creation, registration, or login. All game settings, player names for local matches, and match scores are processed locally on your device and are not transmitted to any private server.

2. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements (such as interstitial ads between game rounds). Google AdMob may collect and process information such as:

  • Device identifiers (such as IDFA, where permitted by user consent).
  • IP address and general coarse location data.
  • Diagnostic and ad performance data (impressions, clicks).

For more information on how Google uses data from apps that partner with them, please visit:
Google Ads & Privacy Policy

3. Children's Privacy

Our applications are intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13. If you believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate measures.

4. User Rights (GDPR & LGPD)

In compliance with global data protection regulations (such as the European Union's GDPR and Brazil's LGPD), you have the right to access, rectify, or request deletion of any personal data associated with your interaction with our services. To exercise these rights, please email us.
